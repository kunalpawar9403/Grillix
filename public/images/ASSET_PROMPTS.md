# GRILLIX AI Visual Asset Workflow & Prompts

This document records the exact prompts, negative prompts, aspect ratios, and color grading parameters used for the GRILLIX food photography asset pipeline.

## System Guidelines
- **Color Grading**: Natural daylight, muted warm tones, deep olive greens (#315B0B, #244507), crisp lime accents (#A8E83C), organic cream backgrounds (#EEF4E3).
- **Lighting**: Soft directional window light, micro water-droplets, realistic subsurface scattering on fresh greens.
- **Style**: High-end editorial culinary photography (Bon Appétit / Kinfolk / Sweetgreen aesthetic). No artificial sheen, no digital watermarks.

---

### 1. `hero-spinach.jpg` (Home Screen Hero)
- **Prompt**: Macro extreme close-up of a vibrant emerald spinach and savoy cabbage leaf with crystal clear glistening dew water droplets, cellular texture visible, rich olive green tones, soft natural morning window light, high-end commercial organic food photography, 8k resolution, cinematic depth of field.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 / 9:16
- **Target Placement**: Home screen top hero section under headline "Eat Fresh, Live Healthy".

### 2. `green-veggie-bowl.jpg` (Signature Item & Product Detail)
- **Prompt**: Overhead flat-lay of a ceramic bowl filled with steamed vibrant broccoli florets, tender baby spinach, sweet green peas, edamame, and fresh mint leaves, dressed with extra virgin olive oil, placed on a weathered white rustic wood surface with scattered spinach leaves and salt crystals.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 & 4:3
- **Target Placement**: Menu item "Greens Bowl" and Product Details hero photograph.

### 3. `rainbow-quinoa-bowl.jpg` (Protein Plates)
- **Prompt**: High-end culinary photography of a warm grain bowl featuring sliced grilled herb chicken breast, fluffy tri-color quinoa, roasted cherry tomatoes, diced avocado, shredded kale, and lime wedge in a shallow speckled bowl.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1
- **Target Placement**: Personalized picks and Cart item "Rainbow Veggie Quinoa Power Bowl".

### 4. `herb-bean-sandwich.jpg` (Handhelds)
- **Prompt**: Artisanal grilled herb and bean patty sandwich on toasted sourdough with grill marks, sliced ripe avocado, roasted red bell peppers, baby arugula, and green tahini sauce on a ceramic plate.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1
- **Target Placement**: AI Personalized Picks recommendation card.

### 5. `fresh-salad-bowl.jpg` (Featured Salads)
- **Prompt**: Top-down view of a colorful Mediterranean salad bowl with organic chickpeas, halved vine cherry tomatoes, crisp leafy greens, diced cucumber, and a fresh lemon wedge on rustic wood.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1
- **Target Placement**: Cart item & Menu featured item.

### 6. Ingredient Assets (`broccoli-ingredient.jpg`, `brassica-ingredient.jpg`, `spinach-ingredient.jpg`)
- **Prompt**: Single organic fresh broccoli floret / wet curled brassica kale leaf / baby spinach leaf isolated on soft natural light surface, dew droplets, crisp botanical detail.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1
- **Target Placement**: Product Details "Ingredients" horizontal showcase.

### 7. Restaurant Interiors (`restaurant-interior-1.jpg`, `restaurant-interior-2.jpg`)
- **Prompt**: Elegant minimalist interior of a modern sustainable farm-to-table restaurant, muted sage and olive green booth seating, blonde natural wood tables, gentle architectural lighting, indoor plants.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:3
- **Target Placement**: Map discovery cards for "Park Meadows" and "Millennium Park".

### 8. Gift Card Promos (`gift-card-fathers-day.jpg`, `gift-card-spring.jpg`)
- **Prompt**: Curated four-grid editorial collage of fresh grilled entrees, green bowls, and pasta dishes with clean typography space for promotional gift card branding.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 / 4:3
- **Target Placement**: Gift cards screen.
